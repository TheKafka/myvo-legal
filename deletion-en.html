How to Delete MYVO Data

Version 1.0-beta1 dated 4 November 2026. Effective 10 November 2026. See also the policy and terms.

New feedback and optional screenshots are stored in Supabase and available to the operator through a private dashboard. No new Google Sheets or Google Drive copies are created. References to Google feedback copies in this document concern previously created working copies only: they remain private and subject to the same retention and deletion rules. This change does not disable Firebase notifications or Gmail email support.

You can request deletion without registering or reinstalling the app.

If MYVO opens

  1. Open Settings → “Delete my data”.
  2. Read the warning and confirm the request.
  3. Connect to the internet so that the request reaches the server. Participation ends once the request is accepted.
  4. Wait for confirmation of completion in MYVO. A pending request means deletion is still in progress. The system retries failed steps. Contact support if deletion does not finish.

If the app has been uninstalled or is unavailable

Write to the support address stating that you want to delete your MYVO guest data, and include the deletion support code you saved beforehand. Support will verify the code, initiate processing and inform you of the result. Sending an email, or its receipt by support, does not itself confirm that deletion is complete.

To save the code in advance, open MYVO Settings → “Deletion support code” → “Copy support code”. Internet access is required to prepare/copy it. Save the code in a safe place outside the app before uninstalling.

Keep it secret: whoever has it can request deletion of the associated guest. The code does not allow sign-in, reading wishes or restoring the record. If you lose both the code and access, contact support. We do not promise to find a random guest record by email address, name or city. Do not send a password, JWT, DNI or other documents for routine code verification.

What is deleted

  • The guest record and associated settings, personal history, personal counters and geographical progress.
  • Associated server records and MYVO push registration, with the exceptions below for deliveries that have already taken place.
  • Your feedback and images, including active working copies in the service Google Sheet and Google Drive folder. The deletion check prevents re-export from an old client.
  • Deletion-code verification data and associated guest access records.

The in-app request also clears MYVO guest data in the accessible installation and requests deletion of the installation identifier from Firebase. Language packs and a technical marker recording completed deletion may remain on the phone; this is not an active guest session. An external request cannot remotely wipe an offline/lost phone or call the Firebase API from it; MYVO's active server-side push registration is nevertheless deleted.

What information may remain

Wishes already delivered to other people, their progress and overall aggregates may remain without a direct link to the deleted sender. Overall collective results are not recalculated retrospectively. A control marker of the deleted feedback operation identifier remains without its text or a direct link to the guest, to prevent the feedback from reappearing; no end date has been set for this marker.

The server confirmation of completed deletion remains without guest/participant identifiers or a list of feedback and is queued for cleanup 30 days after completion. This period does not apply to the local technical marker on the phone. Incomplete requests are retained and retried for fulfilment; that is a different retention period.

Support email and the invitation list are stored separately. They are not automatically deleted together with the guest. In the same email, you can separately request deletion of this information; the policy and applicable statutory exceptions apply to it.

Providers' backups and logs may be cleaned up through their own processes. Firebase publishes a period of up to 180 days for the relevant installation identifier data after the deletion API is called. This is not a general retention period for all data, not a period counted from an email, and not a promise that all Google copies are erased when you press the button.

We limit information retained for a specific legal obligation or an unresolved dispute to the necessary scope and duration. The purpose and corresponding basis are determined for the particular case under section 6 of the policy. The mere presence of such a provision does not mean that all records due for deletion will be retained.

Do you need to wait until the test ends?

No. You can submit a request before automatic cleanup. For an inactive guest, cleanup is triggered after six calendar months; feedback and images are queued for cleanup 90 days after the actual end of their respective period. While the period is open, the countdown from its end has not begun.

We fulfil requests within applicable time limits; Argentine law provides up to five working days for the relevant deletion/rectification, subject to the conditions of the law, while the GDPR provides a general period of one month with the possibility of an extension as provided. A longer period under another regime does not replace an applicable shorter deadline. Technical retries do not remove the obligation to finish processing and inform you of any obstacle.

Deletion is irreversible. Renewed participation creates a new guest. Removing the APK, leaving the Google Play list and the end of a test round do not themselves delete server data.

To withdraw only improvement permission

In Settings, you can choose “Withdraw permission for all previous improvement feedback”. The command covers previous reports and screenshots explicitly submitted for improvements, including active Supabase and Google copies. Support requests, wishes, progress and new materials with a new explicit confirmation remain. An old report’s category alone is not evidence of new consent. A pending state is shown until cleanup finishes; accepting the command is not completion. Failed cleanup is retried. The usual technical pause before cleanup begins is about four minutes plus queue processing, not a promise of a fixed completion time. Without access to the app, contact support; a proportionate check is used to establish the link to the guest. The deletion code is intended for guest deletion, not for reading history.
