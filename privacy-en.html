2. What MYVO does and what anonymity means

In MYVO, you select a wish from a prepared catalogue and send it to a randomly selected eligible participant. The test version offers five categories and 27 wishes. The recipient does not see the sender's name; replying to the same person or starting a free-form conversation is not possible.

A random guest record is created to operate the service. Its identifiers link settings, sends, receipts and progress. Anonymity towards another participant does not mean that service records cannot be linked to a person through additional information. The MYVO operator has administrative access to the necessary service records.

During the test, a wish for which no ordinary recipient is found in time may be delivered to the MYVO operator acting as a special recipient. This does not reveal the sender's name in the wish interface. Separate allocation rules apply to this special recipient; ordinary participants are not given this role.

4. City and optional geolocation

You can search for and select a city manually without location access. If you request a suggestion, MYVO uses the Android location service in low-accuracy mode. Actual accuracy depends on permission, the service and phone settings; this is not a guarantee of a particular radius.

The resulting coordinates are used to determine a city name through the system geocoder. That service may transmit them to its external provider. The search text, country, region where available, and language are then sent to the MYVO server; coordinates on the phone help order the cities found.

For this function, MYVO does not store the device's original coordinates or send them to its own database. The city you confirm is stored. There is no continuous background location tracking. You can revoke permission in Android and continue selecting a city manually. Revoking permission does not delete a previously selected city: you can change it or delete it together with the guest data.

5. Notifications and data on the phone

Push notifications use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. The wish text passes through this service and may be visible on the lock screen depending on Android settings. This is not end-to-end encryption of messages between participants.

Firebase is initialised when the app starts; an installation identifier may be created before permission to display notifications is granted. Declining permission or disabling notifications does not automatically delete a previously created identifier. You can manage notification display in MYVO and Android. After a deletion request from an accessible installation, the app also requests deletion of the installation identifier from Firebase.

The phone stores the guest session, settings, local history, cache and downloaded language packs, as well as draft feedback or feedback awaiting submission. A prepared deletion code is stored separately in secure storage. After the guest is deleted, language packs and a technical marker recording completed deletion may remain on the phone; this is not an active guest session. Some functions and translations are available offline, but wish delivery and feedback submission require internet access.

Google Analytics is disabled, and advertising SDKs and Crashlytics are not included in the current build. MYVO nevertheless processes its own operational events and statistics described in section 3.

Before network submission, the phone stores an unfinished-action record with its confirmation so that a failure or retry does not change the selected purpose. After successful submission and dismissal of the result, the submission record is cleared; an unfinished record remains until its state is resolved or local guest data is cleared. The latest permission-withdrawal receipt may be kept separately. For the location suggestion, only the latest acknowledgement of the explanation is stored on the phone: version, language, text checksum, random attempt ID and phone time, without coordinates or city. A new acknowledgement replaces the previous one; clearing local guest data removes it. This is not a server GPS-movement log.

6. Purposes and legal bases

We limit processing to the purposes described. Accepting the terms of use does not itself constitute consent to any data processing or any future purposes. Android permission also does not replace all necessary information and consent.

Where the GDPR applies, information necessary for the requested guest-session, delivery and personal-progress functions is processed to perform our agreement with you or take pre-contractual steps requested by you — Article 6(1)(b). This basis covers only objectively necessary data, not every operation mentioned in the terms.

Article 6(1)(f) applies to proportionate protection of access, prevention of abuse and prevention of deleted feedback reappearing: our legitimate interest is the security of the service and its participants. Pursuing it is limited to the necessary records, access and retention periods; you have the right to object in the circumstances provided by law. Compliance with applicable obligations relating to data requests is based on Article 6(1)(c).

The optional GPS suggestion and the use of voluntary suggestions/images for general app improvements are based on informed consent — Article 6(1)(a). Information must be provided before the relevant action, separately from acceptance of the general terms. Declining does not block the core functions; withdrawing consent does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful. The minimum actions and information needed to investigate a specific malfunction for which you requested support are processed to perform the agreement under Article 6(1)(b). Use of the same material for general improvements requires the separately specified consent.

To organise your requested current participation in the test, we process an invitation contact and participation information: Article 6(1)(b), as necessary contractual actions or pre-contractual steps requested by you. For Argentina, necessary processing within a contractual relationship applies within the scope of Article 5(2)(d). If you separately ask to be invited to a future round, use of the selected contact for that invitation is based on consent: Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR and Article 5(1) of Argentine law. Consent to the current test does not authorise any future invitations or advertising mailings. You can decline further invitations by writing to support.

Under applicable Argentine law, data necessary for the contractual relationship, including the minimum investigation needed for a malfunction you have asked us to address, is processed within the scope of Article 5(2)(d) of Law 25.326. The corresponding statutory exceptions apply to specific security and data-subject rights obligations, including Article 5(2)(b). The optional location suggestion and use of suggestions/images for general improvements are based on consent under Article 5(1). The European concept of “legitimate interest” is not automatically substituted for an Argentine legal basis.

A separate purpose for the limited retention of specific records is the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Where the GDPR applies, the basis is Article 6(1)(f): an interest in protecting rights, subject to a necessity and balancing assessment; where a directly applicable obligation requires retention of a record, it is Article 6(1)(c). In Argentina, a specific obligation under Article 5(2)(b) or strictly necessary processing within the contractual relationship under Article 5(2)(d) is required; applicable exceptions under Article 16(5) are considered when assessing deletion. Merely mentioning a dispute in these terms does not create a new legal basis or permit indefinite retention of all data. For each exception, the basis, necessary records and review date are determined.

We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you. Random routing, limits and light calculations form part of how MYVO's functions operate.

For requested support, we record which explanation was shown and which action was taken; this does not turn necessary support into consent for general improvements. For voluntary improvement feedback, we record a separate confirmation. Retrying an unfinished submission uses its original confirmation; a changed explanation or a new submission after withdrawal requires the corresponding new action. Changing language alone is not new consent. Minimal verification information is used to carry out the stated purpose and demonstrate or protect the corresponding rights under the grounds in section 6, not for advertising.

7. Who can access information and where it is processed

  • Other participants receive wishes without the sender's name and see collective city lights and the cities of their own exchanges. In a small group, city activity may allow indirect inferences; this does not publish a name, email address or precise GPS location.
  • The MYVO operator and support tools authorised by the operator have administrative access to the extent necessary. Feedback and images are not published to participants.
  • Supabase provides guest authentication, the database, file storage and server functions. MYVO's primary database is configured in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Google Firebase provides notification infrastructure.
  • Google Apps Script, Sheets and Drive remain only for the normal cleanup of previously created private feedback/image working copies; new reports are not exported there. Gmail is used for email support requests. Only the operator’s authorised service accounts have access to previous working copies.
  • Android and geocoding providers are involved when you request a city suggestion.
  • Google Play separately arranges installation and test access under its own rules. Obtaining the app through Play does not sign you into MYVO through Google.

The operator is located in Argentina. Google, Supabase and their infrastructure providers may process information in other countries. The location of the primary database in Germany therefore does not mean that all processing is confined to Germany.

International transfers must have an applicable lawful basis and the necessary safeguards. You can ask support for information about the specific safeguards used. Recognition of a country as adequate applies to the relevant transfer route and does not replace assessment of other transfers. This policy does not mean that any consent authorises all overseas operations or overrides local legal requirements.

Provider information: Supabase Privacy, Supabase DPA, Firebase Privacy, Google Privacy. Their documents do not replace our obligations to you.

If a specific duty arises to disclose data under the law or a lawful request from a competent authority, we assess its applicability and limit disclosure to what is necessary. There is no general permission to publish your feedback or transfer it for advertising.

Cleanup is carried out through a queue with retries. Reaching a deadline or submitting a request does not yet mean that all actions have been completed instantly. If there is a delay, the status must be checked and processing completed.

After a guest is deleted, wishes already delivered to other people, their progress and overall aggregates may remain without a direct link to the deleted sender; collective results are not recalculated retrospectively. This is not a promise that any remaining information is irreversibly anonymous when combined with external information.

Providers' backups and infrastructure logs have their own retention processes. There is no single confirmed maximum period for the entire chain. Firebase publishes a period of up to 180 days for the relevant installation identifier data after its deletion API is called; this is not counted from uninstalling the app or sending an email and does not automatically apply to all Google data.

9. Deletion and other rights

To delete guest data from an accessible app, open Settings → “Delete my data”, confirm the request and wait for the result. If the app is unavailable, use the external deletion instructions. Reinstallation is not required for an external request.

Before uninstalling the app, save the deletion request code. It helps verify a deletion request, but does not allow you to sign in, read wishes or restore a profile. If you lose both access and the code, contact support: we do not promise to find a random record by name, city or email address.

Depending on the applicable law and legal basis, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing or withdraw consent. Requests are accepted using the contacts in section 1. The deletion code is not universal proof of entitlement to receive a copy of data; other requests may require separate proportionate verification. Do not send a password, JWT or documents for routine deletion-code verification.

We respond within the applicable time limits. Under the GDPR, the usual period is one month, with the possibility of an extension and notification as provided by law. Argentine law provides 10 calendar days for access and up to five working days for applicable rectification/deletion/update requests, subject to the conditions of the law. Where a shorter deadline applies, it is not replaced by a longer period under another regime.

You may contact Argentina's AAIP and, where applicable, the competent authority where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place. Contacting us first does not restrict any mandatory right to complain to an authority or court.

In Settings, you can choose “Withdraw permission to use all my previous improvement feedback”. The command covers previous reports and screenshots explicitly submitted for improvements, including active Supabase and Google copies. Support, wishes, progress and new materials with a new explicit confirmation remain. An old report’s category alone is not evidence of new consent. A pending state is shown until cleanup finishes; accepting the command is not completion. Failed cleanup is retried. The usual technical pause before cleanup begins is about four minutes plus queue processing, not a promise of a fixed completion time. Without access to the app, contact support; a proportionate check is used to establish the link to the guest. The deletion code is intended for guest deletion, not for reading history.

10. Security, minors and changes

MYVO uses secure network connections and server access rules. Access to restricted feedback and working copies is limited. Absolute protection against all failures or attacks cannot be promised. We do not ask testers for phone contacts, DNI, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

The test is not intended for anyone under 18. If you become aware of such participation or of unnecessary sensitive information or other people's information being submitted in feedback, contact support so that it can be checked and necessary action taken. The age rule does not itself assert that everyone's age has been technically verified.

We will explain material changes to processing before introducing them where required. New consent is requested separately where applicable; merely reading an updated policy does not constitute consent to any new purposes. Each published version receives a date and number. Applicable mandatory rights remain regardless of language or selected city.
